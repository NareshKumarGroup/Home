    <p>We're excited to share that the <strong>Kumar Research Group</strong> attended the prestigious <strong>32nd IUPAC International Symposium on the Chemistry of Natural Products (ISCNP)</strong> and the <strong>12th IUPAC International Congress on Biodiversity (ICOB)</strong>!</p>

    <p>This international conference brought together experts from around the globe to discuss the latest developments in natural products research and biodiversity. The Kumar group was proud to be part of this important scientific gathering.</p>

    <p><strong>Conference Highlights:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Event:</strong> 32nd IUPAC International Symposium on the Chemistry of Natural Products</li>
        <li><strong>Date:</strong> August 2025</li>
        <li><strong>Location:</strong> International venue</li>
        <li><strong>Focus:</strong> Natural products chemistry and biodiversity research</li>
        <li><strong>Group Participation:</strong> Multiple group members attended and presented</li>
    </ul>

    <p><strong>Scientific Themes Covered:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Isolation & structure elucidation</li>
        <li>Total synthesis & synthetic methodology</li>
        <li>Biosynthesis</li>
        <li>Synthetic biology</li>
        <li>Chemical ecology</li>
        <li>Biologically active molecules & medicinal chemistry</li>
        <li>Informatics & artificial intelligence</li>
    </ul>

    <p>The conference provided an excellent platform for our group to showcase our research in medicinal chemistry and natural products, while also learning about cutting-edge developments in the field. Our participation in this international symposium demonstrates our commitment to staying at the forefront of natural products research.</p>

    <p>We're grateful for the opportunity to connect with researchers from around the world and look forward to the continued collaboration and knowledge exchange that these conferences facilitate.</p>
