    <p><strong>Josh Fleming</strong> from the Kumar Research Group attended the <a href="https://www.shcchem.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Southern Highlands Conference on Heterocyclic Chemistry 2026</a>, held from 30 August to 1 September 2026 at The Sebel Harbourside Kiama (31 Shoalhaven St, Kiama NSW 2533), presenting a poster with <strong>Daniel Guo</strong>. Their poster highlighted a novel approach to combating antimicrobial resistance, showcasing innovative strategies to address this growing challenge.</p>

    <p>The conference featured close-knit discussions on chemistry and its advances, bringing together academics, students, and industry researchers. 🌊</p>

    <figure class="post-figure pitch-figure">
        <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/kiama2026/josh-pitch.jpg" alt="Josh Fleming pitching his poster idea to the audience at Kiama 2026" class="featured-image">
        <figcaption>Josh Fleming pitching his poster idea to the audience.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    <p>Josh is pictured pitching his poster idea to the audience, always a useful test of whether the chemistry survives contact with other chemists. 🧫</p>

    <figure class="post-figure hero-figure">
        <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/kiama2026/topekiama.png" alt="Tope A. Ibisanmi presenting a poster at the Southern Highlands Conference on Heterocyclic Chemistry 2026" class="featured-image">
        <figcaption>Tope A. Ibisanmi with his poster at The Sebel Harbourside Kiama.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    <p><strong>Tope A. Ibisanmi</strong> from the Kumar Research Group also attended the conference, where he presented a poster.</p>

    <p>Well done, Josh and Tope, on representing the group so well on the Kiama harbourfront.</p>
