<div class="post-content hon26-post">
    <p>The twin screens lit up, and our Honours students took the floor. The group is delighted to congratulate <strong>Zainab Yassine</strong> and <strong>Daniel Guo</strong> on engaging talks at the 2026 T3 School of Chemistry Honours final seminars.</p>

    <p>An Honours final seminar is the year in one sitting: the molecules, the data, and the story told when the questions start. Zainab Yassine and Daniel Guo each held that room. Different compounds, one problem. Bacteria that have learned to ignore the antibiotics already in use.</p>

    <div class="talk-pair">
        <article class="talk-blurb">
            <p class="talk-kicker">Quorum sensing</p>
            <h3>Zainab Yassine</h3>
            <p class="talk-title">Synthesis and biological evaluation of dual-acting quorum sensing inhibitors against <em>Pseudomonas aeruginosa</em></p>
            <p>Zainab Yassine's seminar moved from the scale of antibiotic resistance to the organism, a critical priority pathogen that coordinates attack through quorum sensing. The inhibitors under development are built to interrupt that conversation and strike by a second mechanism at the same time, then to be judged in biological assays.</p>
        </article>
        <article class="talk-blurb">
            <p class="talk-kicker">Peptide mimics</p>
            <h3>Daniel Guo</h3>
            <p>Daniel Guo is designing <strong>antimicrobial peptide mimics</strong>. Daniel Guo took the room through structure–activity relationships and optimization: which changes to a scaffold do nothing, and which changes lift activity. The active compounds presented improved on the previous generation, with a clear step forward against Gram-negative <em>E. coli</em>.</p>
        </article>
    </div>


    <p>Both seminars closed with the same thanks. Zainab Yassine and Daniel Guo thanked supervisors <strong>Professor Naresh Kumar</strong> and <strong>Tsz Tin (Tom) Yu</strong> for unwavering support throughout the project, and for the opportunity to work with world-renowned Professor Naresh. Zainab Yassine thanked mentor <strong>Rasel Ahmed Khan</strong> for being the best mentor over the year. Daniel Guo thanked mentor <strong>Samuel (Sam) Attard</strong> in the same way.</p>

    <p>Congratulations to Zainab Yassine and Daniel Guo. The Kumar Group is proud of the science told in these seminars. The future of antimicrobial drug development is bright with these brilliant minds.</p>
