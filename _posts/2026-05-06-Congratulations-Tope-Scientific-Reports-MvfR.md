    <p>Congratulations to Tope A. Ibisanmi, Xiaotao Jiang, Rasel Ahmed Khan, Tsz Tin Yu, Mark Willcox, and Naresh Kumar on the publication of their paper in <em>Scientific Reports</em>.</p>

    <p>In this work, they present an integrative computational strategy to prioritize candidate inhibitors of MvfR  key regulator of quorum sensing and biofilm formation in multidrug-resistant <em>Pseudomonas aeruginosa</em>—bringing together genomic context from clinical isolates, modelling, docking, phylogenetics, ADMET profiling, long-timescale molecular dynamics, binding–free-energy analysis, and DFT-based quantum chemistry.</p>

    <p><strong>Open access:</strong> Read the full article on Nature: <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-50613-z" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio)</a> · DOI <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-50613-z" target="_blank" rel="noopener">10.1038/s41598-026-50613-z</a> (published online 5 May 2026).</p>
