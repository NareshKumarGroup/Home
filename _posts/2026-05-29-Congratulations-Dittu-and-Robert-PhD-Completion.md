    <h3>Their Research</h3>

    <p><strong>Dr Dittu Suresh</strong> completed his PhD on dual-action antimicrobials, with a focus on quorum sensing inhibitors and small-molecule antimicrobial peptide mimics. His work tackled one of the most pressing challenges in modern medicine, antimicrobial resistance, by designing compounds that can disrupt bacterial communication and combat resistant pathogens through multiple mechanisms. Dittu's contributions reflect the creativity and rigour that define the best of medicinal chemistry research.</p>

    <p><strong>Dr Robert Rourke</strong> completed his PhD in organic synthesis, developing novel molecules through careful design and hands-on synthetic chemistry. His work strengthened the group's foundation in building new chemical scaffolds, the kind of skilled, meticulous science that underpins drug discovery across every project in the lab.</p>
