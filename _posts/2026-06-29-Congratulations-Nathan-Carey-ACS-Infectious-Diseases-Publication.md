    <p>We're delighted to congratulate <strong>Nathan Carey</strong> and co-authors on the publication of a new review in <em>ACS Infectious Diseases</em>—a leading, high-impact journal at the forefront of infectious disease research.</p>

    <p>The article, <strong>A Review of Contact Lens-Associated Infections and Biomaterial-Based Solutions</strong>, surveys the clinical burden of contact lens-related infections and the biomaterial strategies being developed to prevent them. From microbial keratitis and biofilm formation on lens surfaces to advances in antimicrobial coatings, surface modifications, and next-generation lens materials, the review brings together current understanding and emerging solutions in a field where patient safety and innovation go hand in hand.</p>

    <p>Publishing in <em>ACS Infectious Diseases</em> is a significant achievement. The journal is widely recognised for rigorous, impactful work at the interface of chemistry, materials science, and infectious disease.</p>

    <p><strong>Publication details:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Journal:</strong> ACS Infectious Diseases (Article ASAP)</li>
        <li><strong>Title:</strong> A Review of Contact Lens-Associated Infections and Biomaterial-Based Solutions</li>
        <li><strong>Authors:</strong> Nathan Carey, Desie Kasew Assefa, Yalewayker Asrat, Muhammad Yasir, Naresh Kumar, and Mark Willcox</li>
        <li><strong>DOI:</strong> <a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00072" target="_blank" rel="noopener">10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00072</a></li>
    </ul>

    <p>Congratulations, Nathan, on this outstanding achievement! The whole Kumar Research Group is proud of your work and excited to see the impact of this review in the field.</p>
