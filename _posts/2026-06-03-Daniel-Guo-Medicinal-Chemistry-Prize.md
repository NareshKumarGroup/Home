    <p>The Kumar Group is delighted to congratulate <strong>Daniel Guo</strong> on winning the <strong>Prestigious Medicinal Chemistry Prize for Best Performance in the Level 3 Medicinal Chemistry course</strong> at the School of Chemistry, Faculty of Science, UNSW Sydney.</p>

    <p>This is an exceptional achievement. Daniel was the <strong>only student in the cohort to achieve a High Distinction (85%+) in every single assessment item throughout the course</strong>. Such a remarkable record reflects not only his outstanding academic ability but also his dedication, consistency, and commitment to excellence.</p>

    <p>Medicinal chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that integrates chemistry, biology, pharmacology, and drug discovery to advance the development of new therapeutics. Daniel's exceptional performance across all assessments demonstrates a deep understanding of these disciplines and an unwavering commitment to academic excellence.</p>

    <p>We are incredibly proud to have Daniel as a member of the Kumar Group. His hard work, enthusiasm for learning, and pursuit of excellence have been recognised through this well deserved award.</p>

    <p>Please join us in congratulating Daniel on this outstanding accomplishment. We look forward to seeing his continued success in medicinal chemistry and beyond.</p>

    <p><strong>Congratulations, Daniel, on this remarkable achievement and well deserved recognition.</strong></p>
