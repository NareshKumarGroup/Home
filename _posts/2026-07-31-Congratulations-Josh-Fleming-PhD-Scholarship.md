    <p>We are delighted to congratulate <strong>Josh Fleming</strong> on receiving a PhD scholarship — a well-earned milestone and the start of an exciting new chapter in his research career.</p>

    <p>Josh’s dedication throughout his Honours year stood out at every turn: curiosity that kept pushing the science forward, and a determination to see projects through. That same commitment carried his Honours research into a published paper, and it is exactly the foundation a strong PhD is built on.</p>

    <p>None of this happens alone. Josh has been fortunate to work with an outstanding supervisory team, and their guidance, encouragement, and shared commitment to excellence have helped shape this next step. We are grateful for the mentorship that surrounds him as he begins his doctoral studies.</p>

    <p>The whole Kumar Research Group is proud of you, Josh. Congratulations on your PhD scholarship — we cannot wait to see what you discover next.</p>
