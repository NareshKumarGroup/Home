    <p>We're thrilled to share that <strong>Alyssa Morellini</strong> had an incredible opportunity to attend and volunteer at the <strong>32nd IUPAC International Symposium on the Chemistry of Natural Products</strong>!</p>

    <p>It was so fascinating to hear about all of the upcoming research in the field of natural products and the wider chemistry space</p>

    <p>Alyssa also had an amazing time sharing her Honours thesis on the design and synthesis of novel FLT3 inhibitors targeting acute myeloid leukaemia. It was a fantastic opportunity to discuss her research with others and she looks forward to  future opportunities</p>

    <p><em>"Thank you so much to every member and collaborator of the Kumar group and the Lowy Cancer Research Centre for your support and guidance along the way 🙂"</em> - Alyssa Morellini</p>

    <p>Congratulations, Alyssa, on this fantastic achievement and for representing our research group so well at this prestigious international symposium!</p>
