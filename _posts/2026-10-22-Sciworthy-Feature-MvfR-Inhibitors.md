    <p>One of our papers published in <em>Scientific Reports</em> has been featured in <strong>Sciworthy</strong>, in the article <em>"Scientists found a compound that can disarm a drug-resistant bacterium"</em> by Halimat Chisom Atanda.</p>

    <p>The article highlights our study, <strong>Multimodal computational discovery of MvfR inhibitors targeting quorum sensing in multi-drug-resistant <em>Pseudomonas aeruginosa</em></strong>, which used genome data from recent clinical isolates, AlphaFold2 protein modelling and drug-likeness screening to identify compounds that could disarm <em>P. aeruginosa</em> by blocking MvfR, a core protein in its quorum-sensing system.</p>

    <p><strong>Authors:</strong> Tope Abraham Ibisanmi, Xiaotao Jiang, Rasel Ahmed Khan, Tsz Tin (Tom) Yu, Mark Willcox and Naresh Kumar</p>

    <p>It is exciting to see our research reaching a wider audience. Congratulations to <strong>Tope</strong> and all the authors, and special thanks to Professor Mark Willcox and Professor Naresh Kumar for making this research possible.</p>
