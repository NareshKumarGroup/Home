    <p>🏆 <strong>Prizes & Applause!</strong></p>
    
    <p>A huge congratulations to <strong>Rasel Khan</strong> (Kumar group) for winning a poster prize at the <strong>Southern Highlands Conference on Heterocyclic Chemistry</strong>! 🌟</p>

    <p>His outstanding work on <strong>quorum sensing inhibitors</strong> not only showcases cutting-edge science in the fight against bacterial resistance, but also proves that beautiful molecules and elegant design truly go hand in hand. 🧪✨</p>

    <p><strong>Conference Details:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Event:</strong> Southern Highlands Conference on Heterocyclic Chemistry</li>
        <li><strong>Date:</strong> August 31st - September 2nd, 2025</li>
        <li><strong>Achievement:</strong> Poster Prize Winner</li>
        <li><strong>Research Focus:</strong> Quorum Sensing Inhibitors</li>
        <li><strong>Research Group:</strong> Kumar Group, UNSW Sydney</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Well done, Rasel! Your dedication, creativity, and clarity in presenting complex chemistry are truly inspiring. Here's to more breakthroughs and well-deserved recognition in your research journey! 👏👏👏</p>

    <p>This achievement highlights the innovative research being conducted in our group on bacterial resistance and the development of novel therapeutic strategies. Rasel's work on quorum sensing inhibitors represents a promising approach to combating antibiotic-resistant bacterial infections.</p>
